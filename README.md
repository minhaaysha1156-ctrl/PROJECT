### Project Description
SheShield is a comprehensive women's safety platform designed to bridge the gap between digital protection and real-world safety. It provides a secure environment for reporting abuse, accessing resources, and triggering immediate emergency assistance, specifically addressing threats like deepfakes and harassment.

### The Problem statement
Women face increasingly complex threats in both the physical and digital worlds, including deepfakes, image manipulation, and online harassment. Existing solutions are often fragmented, leaving victims without a unified platform for reporting incidents or accessing timely support.

### The Solution
SheShield offers a unified platform that combines immediate emergency response features with tools to combat digital abuse. By integrating secure reporting, a resource hub for legal/medical aid, and specific support for deepfake victims, it empowers women to protect themselves effectively.

## Features

- **Emergency SOS**: A prominent, immediate-access button triggered for urgent assistance.
- **Report Abuse**: A secure and confidential channel to report incidents of digital or physical harassment.
- **Deepfake Protection**: Detailed resources and support specifically for victims of image manipulation and deepfake threats.
- **Resource Hub**: A curated list of legal, medical, and psychological support services.
